HOW TO FLY

  • Tap, click or press Space to flap. Every flap is one beat of lift.
  • Thread the gap between the glowing monoliths. Brushing an edge is a near miss — you get a spark, not a crash.
  • Watch for shards. The black crystals with a white‑hot core drift between the gates and they will end your run. There is always a clear line past them.
  • Gates change as you climb. They start sliding, then offset into diagonal slots, then arrive in tight pairs, then breathe open and shut.
  • Every 10 gates the world shifts: dawn meadow, coast, canyon, night, aurora.
  • P or Esc pauses. R restarts. F shows the frame rate.
