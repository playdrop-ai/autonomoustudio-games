# Flighty Saucer — Art Direction

- Fantasy: pilot a charming tiny UFO through monumental, dreamlike low-poly
  landscapes with split-second one-tap corrections.
- Identity: a silver-lilac saucer with a dark teal underside, orange illuminated
  rim, and glass dome containing a mint-green alien pilot; curious, nimble,
  optimistic.
- Setting and mood: golden dawn over stylized green hills, blue water, faceted
  mountains, soft clouds, and enormous warm-stone monolith gates; airy,
  adventurous, gently surreal.
- Palette: Cloud White `#FFFFFF`, Dawn Peach `#FFD6C6`, Signal Orange `#FF8355`,
  Saucer Lilac `#B8BCD9`, Alien Mint `#65D7C2`, Deep Teal `#0B3C42`.
- UI material language: translucent smoked glass, broad rounded corners,
  pill-shaped white buttons, bold geometric display type, crisp white labels,
  and restrained orange glow.

The shipped procedural Three.js environment is the 3D backdrop. Listing art
should heighten the same saucer, monoliths, dawn palette, and low-poly forms
without inventing characters, weapons, currencies, or meta-game screens.

The canonical listing-logo composition is the centered Gate Crest: ivory
`FLIGHTY` above the premium-rendered shipped UFO, coral-orange `SAUCER` below,
deep-teal dimensional edging, small star sparks, and a cyan tractor-light
column framed by the orange lips of a top and bottom monolith gate. Keep the
complete lockup centered in both portrait and landscape heroes, never in a
corner.
