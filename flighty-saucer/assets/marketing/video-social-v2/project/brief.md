# Flighty Saucer hero-led video brief

## Goal

Prove the one-tap flight mechanic immediately after a short identity beat, then close on the same protected hero art.

## Destinations

- Portrait social short: 9:16, 1080x1920, en-US.
- Landscape trailer cut: 16:9, 1920x1080, en-US.
- Target duration: 11.8 seconds each.

## Locked creative

- Protected portrait hero: `assets/marketing/playdrop/hero-portrait-9x16-v3.png`.
- Protected landscape hero: `assets/marketing/playdrop/hero-landscape-16x9-v3.png`.
- Use each hero unchanged for the 0.8 second opening and 1.5 second closing beat.
- Use only real, HUD-free gameplay from the ratio-native source capture.
- Keep the approved generated hand and radial feedback already rendered by the live preview.
- Fire one visible hand press and ring for every autoplay thrust throughout the gameplay beat.
- Add no captions, CTA, regenerated gameplay, crop, padding, stretch, or decorative overlay.

## Edit

1. Identity: exact hero, 0.8 seconds, silent.
2. Mechanic: source gameplay 0.0 to 9.5 seconds at 1x speed with synchronized game audio.
3. Closing identity: exact hero, 1.5 seconds, silent.

The 9.5 second source cutoff keeps the take inside the clean, reviewed gameplay window.
