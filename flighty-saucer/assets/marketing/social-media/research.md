# Flighty Saucer launch research

Research completed on 2026-07-30 before writing the campaign copy.

## Comparable successful games

| Game | Current positioning signal | Useful lesson for Flighty Saucer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Flappy Dunk](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flappy-dunk/id1235581326) | "Tap and bounce to a high score" leads with the literal verb and payoff. | Put the tap mechanic and score chase ahead of lore. |
| [Helix Jump](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/helix-jump/id1345968745) | "Reach the bottom of the tower!" gives one immediate goal, then explains the hazard rule. | State the goal and danger in plain language. |
| [Crossy Road](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/crossy-road/id924373886) | "Endless Arcade Hopper" combines a familiar arcade loop with a distinctive low-poly identity and many worlds. | Let the low-poly visual identity and environment variety carry the emotion. |
| [Tiny Wings](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiny-wings/id417817520) | "A dream of flying" gives an emotional flight fantasy to a simple one-touch loop. | Balance literal control copy with a light, uplifting flying fantasy. |
| [Jetpack Joyride](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jetpack-joyride/id457446957) | One-touch control, varied hazards, acceleration, and higher scores are all explicit. | Sell the movement, surprise obstacles, and escalating score chase. |

The shared competitor pattern is direct and compact: verb, hazard, payoff. Flighty Saucer's differentiators are the tiny UFO character, synchronized visible tap instruction, surprising crystal hazards, and five vividly different low-poly environments.

## Current platform guidance

### YouTube

- [YouTube Shorts guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en-GB) classifies square or vertical uploads up to three minutes as Shorts. The approved 11.8-second 9:16 master is comfortably native.
- [YouTube upload guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407?hl=en) says tags mainly help with misspellings and otherwise play a minimal discovery role. The package therefore uses a focused search-tag list and puts the real keywords in the title and description.
- [YouTube search guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16090438?hl=en) emphasizes relevance, engagement, and quality. The title, description, and visible gameplay all describe the same one-tap UFO mechanic.
- The trailer publishes before the Short so the Short can point to it as a related video.

### TikTok

- [TikTok creative best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en) recommends a 9:16 canvas, strong first-six-second hook, proposition in the first three seconds, sound, and safe-zone-aware creative.
- [TikTok hyper-casual game guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/tiktok-hyper-casual-game-creative-tips/pc/en) recommends clear gameplay, distinct creative patterns, and current community research rather than one generic formula.
- [TikTok Trends guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-use-trends) makes hashtag trends region and time dependent. This package uses stable, relevant category hashtags and avoids `#fyp` stuffing. Trends should be checked again at publication time.

### Instagram

- [Meta Reels guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads) recommends native 9:16 video with audio and key creative elements inside the safe zone.
- [Instagram boost requirements](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/570215404599013) require full-screen 9:16 Reels under 90 seconds.
- The assets and copy are ready, but publication is excluded while the known PlayDrop Instagram account remains disabled.

### Pinterest

- [Pinterest creative best practices](https://business.pinterest.com/creative-best-practices/) recommends vertical 2:3 creative, visual-first composition, the product or action front and center, concise messaging, and a clear story.
- [Pinterest product specifications](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-product-specs) recommend 1000x1500 static Pins, note that the first 40 title characters are most visible, and use descriptions for relevance.
- The package therefore includes a 2:3 video Pin, five distinct 2:3 image Pins, keyword-led titles, descriptive text, and no hashtag block.

### X

- [X organic best practices](https://business.x.com/en/basics/organic-best-practices) recommend concise conversational copy, minimal or no hashtags, media, and video at 15 seconds or less.
- [X video specifications](https://business.x.com/en/help/campaign-setup/creative-ad-specifications) support H.264/AAC, 16:9 at 1920x1080, and up to 60 fps.
- The package uses the approved 11.8-second landscape master, no hashtags, one clear message, descriptive alt text, and the tracked game link in the first reply.

## Copy decision

Primary promise:

> Every tap bumps a tiny UFO upward. Dodge the unexpected, cross five vivid worlds, and beat your best.

The copy avoids unsupported claims, fake urgency, generic virality tags, competitor names, and instructions that do not match the game. It uses "free in your browser, no download" only on surfaces where the destination can be stated clearly.
