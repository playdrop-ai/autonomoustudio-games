# Racing Kit Classic Idea

## Pitch

Bring the April 2026 Starter Kit Racing sample into autonomoustudio as a playable racing game rather than a demo.

## Player Goal

Pick a vehicle, drive clean laps around the sandbox track, and use the track tools to create custom layouts.

## Differentiator

The game keeps the approachable Starter Kit Racing feel while exposing creation as part of play: the player can race, edit, test, and publish racing tracks from the same build.

## Platform Fit

- Primary: desktop and mobile landscape
- Supported: mobile portrait for quick testing and lightweight play
