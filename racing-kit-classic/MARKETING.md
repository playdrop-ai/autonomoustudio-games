# Pocket Circuit Marketing

## Listing Positioning

Pocket Circuit is a bright, toy-scale racing game where players drift tiny low-poly vehicles around a compact circuit, swap rides, and remix the track with the built-in editor.

## Store Copy

Drift tiny trucks around a pocket-sized circuit, then rebuild the track and race your own layout.

## Visual Assets

- App icon: `listing/icon.png`
- Landscape hero: `listing/hero-landscape.png`
- Portrait hero: `listing/hero-portrait.png`
- Source art: `assets/marketing/playdrop/`

The icon uses a nicer rendering of the real yellow truck as the dominant subject for stronger contrast at small sizes. The hero images are AI-generated PlayDrop listing art with the `POCKET CIRCUIT` logo integrated in the artwork, based on the real game capture, GLB-derived vehicle references, a complete landscape circuit composition, faster racing motion cues, and the title-forward treatment used by Starfold, Team Sweeper, and Tomb Sweeper.
