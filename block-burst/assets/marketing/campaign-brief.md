# Block Burst campaign brief

Status: production lock for owner and marketing-director review.

## Objective

Turn the released Block Burst build into a complete, reusable launch campaign
for the PlayDrop listing, AppLovin, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest, and
X. Every claim must be visible in the shipped `1.0.7` game or its real preview
capture.

## Audience

- casual puzzle players who understand drag-and-drop block placement instantly;
- score chasers who enjoy planning several moves ahead;
- players attracted to bright, tactile clears and short satisfying sessions;
- fans of block puzzles who want a fast browser game with no download.

## Position

Block Burst is the jewel-grid score chase where every clean placement can set up
the next bright payoff.

## Primary player promise

Drag a piece. Burst a line. Keep the grid alive long enough to chain the next
combo and beat your best.

## Core story

| Beat | Truth | Player-facing message | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core action | Drag one of three pieces onto the 8x8 grid to complete a row or column. | `DRAG. DROP. BURST.` | The shipped preview and normal-game trailer show complete drags, releases, placements, and line bursts. |
| Tension | Every placement changes the room available for the remaining pieces. | `PLAN EVERY PIECE` | The normal-game trailer holds on a developing board through several distinct setup moves. |
| Payoff | Consecutive clears raise the combo and trigger brighter multi-line effects. | `CHAIN BIG COMBOS` | Both revised masters finish on a countable six-line clear with two shipped bombs; the landscape session also shows a separate mid-session row clear. |
| Goal | A completed run can set a new saved best and highest-score leaderboard result. | `BEAT YOUR BEST` | The shipped HUD, result panel, saved best, and `highest_score` leaderboard support the claim. |

These four headlines are locked verbatim. Each marketing screenshot must render
one headline exactly once, with no extra body copy.

## Truth brief by screenshot

### 1. `DRAG. DROP. BURST.`

- Show: an approved white cartoon hand dragging one jewel-block piece toward a
  nearly complete row, with the first warm-gold fragments appearing at commit.
- May include: the actual seven-color jewel palette, the navy 8x8 grid, three
  tray pieces, a normal row or column clear, and the Cinder Plum environment.
- Must not include: rotating pieces, a timer, enemies, characters, coins,
  level numbers, fake rewards, or a device frame.

### 2. `PLAN EVERY PIECE`

- Show: a readable board with constrained open space and three materially
  different pieces waiting below or beside it.
- May include: a subtle projected placement glow and a calm planning moment.
- Must not imply: a puzzle level with one prescribed solution, piece rotation,
  a hold slot, or a timer.

### 3. `CHAIN BIG COMBOS`

- Show: a truthful crossed row-and-column clear or successive clear with the
  game-supported `COMBO x3` payoff as visual evidence.
- May include: restrained square fragments, warm white-gold light, and the
  normal jewel blocks.
- Must not include: bombs, rockets, lasers, unrelated power-ups, or a combo
  higher than the real game can produce in ordinary play.

### 4. `BEAT YOUR BEST`

- Show: the board surviving under pressure while a strong clear opens space,
  with the score-chase feeling carried by composition rather than invented UI.
- May include: the shipped score/best hierarchy and the real Cinder Plum world.
- Must not include: cash prizes, tournaments, ranks, leagues, trophies that
  look like earned inventory, or a fake victory screen.

## Visual direction

- Preserve the protected Block Burst identity: white-and-gold block lettering,
  jewel-bright beveled blocks, Cinder Plum background, navy grid, and warm-gold
  burst light.
- Use complete edge-to-edge compositions with no phone frame, decorative
  border, browser chrome, generic app-store chrome, or watermark.
- Keep the board or core burst as the largest visual proof after the headline.
- Use bold white dimensional headline lettering with restrained gold edges,
  clearly related to the protected title but not a copy of the title lockup.
- Give every frame a different focal state and color balance.
- Keep important art and copy safe for platform-applied rounded masks.

## Video plan

### Landscape trailer

- Destination: PlayDrop campaign review, YouTube, and X.
- Export: 1920x1080 H.264 MP4, 60 fps, AAC stereo, exactly 17 seconds.
- Opening proof: the first legal move and burst begin inside the first three
  seconds.
- Story: seven legal placements on one persistent board -> opening clear ->
  repair and corner setup -> a second real row clear -> support repair -> one
  final 3x3 placement completes three rows and three columns and detonates two
  bombs.
- Pacing: one continuous 1x native capture with synchronized SFX. There are no
  gameplay cuts, board resets, repeated moves, or speed changes.
- Caption plates: `DRAG. DROP. BURST.`, `PLAN EVERY PIECE`,
  `CHAIN BIG COMBOS`, and `BEAT YOUR BEST`.
- The clean PlayDrop landscape listing uses the same session without captions.

### Portrait teaser

- Destinations: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Stories.
- Export: 1080x1920 H.264 MP4, 60 fps, AAC stereo, exactly 12 seconds.
- Opening proof: immediate real gameplay; the first legal move creates a clear
  and bomb burst inside the opening beat.
- Focus: five legal placements on one board, including a repair and two compact
  setup moves, then one 3x3 placement that clears six lines and detonates two
  bombs.
- Pacing: the same continuous 1x native gameplay take as the approved AppLovin
  video, with social caption plates and no hero insert, cut, reset, or time
  warp. The clean PlayDrop portrait listing is a refreshed take with the same
  five-move story captured from the debris-enabled `1.0.7` build.
- Sound-off message: `DRAG. DROP. BURST.` -> `PLAN EVERY PIECE` ->
  `CHAIN BIG COMBOS` -> `BEAT YOUR BEST`.

### AppLovin cut

- Destination: AppLovin paid acquisition.
- Export: 1080x1920 H.264 MP4, 30 fps, AAC stereo, exactly 12 seconds.
- Story: immediate legal opening clear -> a repair placement on the same board
  -> two compact corner-building moves with one normal tray refresh -> readable
  planning hold -> one 3x3 move completes three rows and three columns,
  detonates two bombs, and clears six counted lines -> separate protected end
  card supplied to the network.
- The complete video is one continuous native-captured gameplay take at 1x:
  no hero insert, gameplay cut, board reset, repeated move, or time warp.
- The ending uses the standard truthful listing-preview contract for a
  reproducible payoff. It remains playable and uses the shipped placement,
  line-clear, bomb, score, and celebration rules without modification. The move
  commits at approximately 9.8 seconds, about two seconds before the video
  ends.
- AppLovin receives a separate, byte-identical protected portrait hero as its
  end card. The video contains no fake CTA, install button, rating, badge, or
  duplicated network metadata.
