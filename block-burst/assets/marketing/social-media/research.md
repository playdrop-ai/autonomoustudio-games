# Block Burst social research

Research was completed before production on August 1, 2026.

## Category signal

Current US App Store listings for Block Blast!, Woodoku, Blockudoku, 1010!, and
Block Puzzle Jewel consistently lead with the literal placement mechanic, a
visible clear, a combo or streak payoff, and a score goal. The strongest frames
keep the board large and use one short message at a time. Detailed evidence and
links are in `../storefront-research.md`.

## Internal production precedent

Nuts and Bolts supplied the four-beat tactile-story pattern. Solitaire supplied
ratio-native store and social packaging. Flighty Saucer supplied the current
real-gameplay video, AppLovin end-card, manifest, and director-handoff structure.

## Decision

The campaign owns a direct promise instead of generic brain-training language:

1. `DRAG. DROP. BURST.`
2. `PLAN EVERY PIECE`
3. `CHAIN BIG COMBOS`
4. `BEAT YOUR BEST`

No copy claims daily puzzles, levels, offline support, no ads, piece rotation,
multiplayer, tournaments, currencies, or rewards absent from the shipped game.
