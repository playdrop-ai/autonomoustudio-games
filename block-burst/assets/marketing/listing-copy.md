## Expanded product description

Build the line. Make it burst. Set up the next one.

Drag one of three jewel-bright pieces onto the 8x8 grid. Complete a full row or
column to clear space in a flash of warm-gold light, then use the room you made
to keep the run alive.

Every placement matters. Leave space for awkward shapes, clear again before the
combo fades, and turn careful planning into bigger multi-line payoffs. When one
block ruins the perfect setup, the hammer can open a path. When the board finally
locks, one rewarded revive can keep a strong run going.

Chase a new personal best and post your completed score to the highest-score
leaderboard. Block Burst is easy to understand in one move and tense for as long
as you can keep finding space.
