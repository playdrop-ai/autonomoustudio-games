# Block Burst storefront research

Research completed on August 1, 2026 before campaign production. Store signals
were checked against current US App Store pages, while PlayDrop's approved Nuts
and Bolts, Solitaire, Flighty Saucer, and Starfold packages supplied the internal
quality and structure precedent.

## Shared category pattern

The strongest category listings follow the same sequence:

1. show the drag-and-place mechanic immediately;
2. show how lines disappear or the board opens;
3. show a combo, streak, or stronger clear;
4. close on score mastery or replay value.

They also keep copy short, make the grid the largest recognisable object, and
use high-contrast pieces against a quiet surface. Weak listings add generic
brain-training language, too many game modes, or screenshots that repeat the
same board with a different headline.

## Internal precedent

- Nuts and Bolts proves that a tactile verb, a planning message, a satisfying
  payoff, and a mastery goal make a coherent four-frame story.
- Solitaire proves that one protected identity system can support native
  portrait, landscape, 3:4, and 2:3 compositions without frames or filler.
- Flighty Saucer proves the current handoff structure: protected identity,
  real gameplay, ratio-native capture, separate AppLovin end card, channel copy,
  technical manifest, and combined review composite.
- Starfold remains the minimum quality bar for a four-message PlayDrop listing.

## Campaign decision

Block Burst will lead with `DRAG. DROP. BURST.` because the action and payoff are
understood together. `PLAN EVERY PIECE` introduces tension, `CHAIN BIG COMBOS`
shows the distinctive payoff, and `BEAT YOUR BEST` gives the run a durable goal.
No campaign frame will advertise daily content, multiple modes, piece rotation,
offline play, or a Sudoku mechanic.
