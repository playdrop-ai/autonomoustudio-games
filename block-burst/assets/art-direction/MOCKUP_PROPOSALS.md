# Block Burst Portrait Mockup Proposals

These are primary-surface art explorations, not shipped gameplay or listing art.
Each proposal uses the same gameplay contract so the visual directions can be
compared directly:

- exact 8 by 8 board
- score `12,480` and best `18,950`
- hammer count `2`
- exactly three tray pieces
- one crossed row-and-column burst
- normal blocks only
- full-screen mobile portrait composition

## 1. Prismatic Demolition

File: `mockup-prismatic-demolition.png`

Prompt: Create an implementation-feasible Phaser 2D portrait gameplay screen
with a thick smoked-glass board, clearly inset cells, luminous translucent resin
blocks, restrained cyan circuit seams, coral flecks, and a hot white-gold crossed
line fracture. Add compact square shards, one thin shockwave, magnetic tray docks,
and the existing simple hammer symbol. Keep the screen readable and avoid title,
marketing copy, special blocks, device framing, photorealism, and particle clutter.

## 2. Kinetic Toybox

File: `mockup-kinetic-toybox.png`

Prompt: Create an implementation-feasible Phaser 2D portrait gameplay screen
with a chunky graphite molded-plastic board, rounded inset cells, satin toy-block
pieces, a cool graphite and muted-teal play surface, coral and mint edge accents,
and a compact white-yellow pressure pop at the crossed clear. Anchor the three
pieces in shallow molded docks. Avoid gemstone materials, candy imagery, special
blocks, device framing, photorealism, and excessive confetti.

## 3. Signal Pop

File: `mockup-signal-pop.png`

Prompt: Create an implementation-feasible Phaser 2D portrait gameplay screen
with a near-black graphic field, off-white, cyan, and coral edge planes, a thick
off-black board, thin desaturated-blue cell outlines, flat enamel blocks with one
hard facet and offset shadow, and a disciplined graphic crossed-line impact using
off-white lines, a coral center, and a cyan shock ring. Use three outlined tray
docks. Avoid glossy gems, realistic materials, soft atmospheric glow, special
blocks, device framing, and particle clutter.

## 4. Pixel Quarry 2D

File: `mockup-pixel-quarry-2d.png`

Prompt: Create an original high-resolution 2D pixel-art mining and block-crafting
gameplay screen from a direct overhead camera. Use an exact 8 by 8 dark pixel-stone
board, colorful original mineral-tile patterns, a warm mined-block crossed-line
burst, and three stone inventory docks. Keep the HUD contract and avoid copied
game textures, recognizable block replicas, characters, special blocks, device
framing, and excessive pixel noise.

## 5. Voxel Quarry 3D

File: `mockup-voxel-quarry-3d.png`

Prompt: Translate Pixel Quarry into an original stylized 3D voxel gameplay screen
while preserving the same direct overhead camera, axis-aligned square board,
gameplay state, HUD, and tray composition. Use shallow raised mineral cubes,
ambient occlusion, carved stone cells, warm lantern edges, and compact cubic clear
debris. Avoid copied game textures, recognizable block replicas, characters,
special blocks, isometric rotation, device framing, and excessive debris.
