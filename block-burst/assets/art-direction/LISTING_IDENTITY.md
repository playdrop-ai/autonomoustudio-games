# Block Burst Listing Identity

Status: selected for the PlayDrop icon and hero refresh.

## Identity Promise

Show the core rule in one glance: complete a straight row or column of chunky
jewel blocks and watch it burst from the center. Use a deep charcoal and muted
Cinder Plum field, white-gold clear light, compact square debris, and restrained
coral and cyan seam accents. The hammer is a secondary supporting tool, never the
main fantasy.

## App Icon

File: `../marketing/playdrop/icon.png`

Prompt direction: a text-free square icon with one straight multicolor line of
large beveled blocks, a central yellow block bursting with a white-gold core, and
only enough square debris to communicate motion at thumbnail size. No board,
special blocks, hammer, or blue background.

## Portrait Hero

File: `../marketing/playdrop/hero-portrait.png`

Prompt direction: a separately composed 9:16 key art image with the exact title
`BLOCK BURST`, crossed horizontal and vertical jewel-block lines clearing from a
white-gold intersection, and the silver-and-gold hammer kept secondary near the
lower-right edge. No gameplay HUD, screenshot frame, or unsupported mechanic.

## Landscape Hero

File: `../marketing/playdrop/hero-landscape.png`

Prompt direction: a separately composed 16:9 key art image with the exact title
`BLOCK BURST` in the left third and the crossed-line clear occupying the right
two-thirds. Match the portrait materials and lighting without cropping it.

## References

- `mockup-background-cinder-plum.png` supplied the truthful gameplay materials,
  palette, line-clear effect, and hammer design.
- Starfold's approved PlayDrop identity package was used only as a quality and
  hierarchy benchmark.

All three final images were generated with built-in ImageGen, inspected at full
size and thumbnail size, then exported to their canonical PlayDrop dimensions.
