# Starfold marketing media

| Folder | Destination | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `playdrop/` | PlayDrop game detail and catalogue | Two heroes, four text screenshots per orientation, and one no-text gameplay capture per orientation. Portrait media is 9:16 and landscape media is 16:9. Only this folder is referenced by `catalogue.json`. |
| `app-store-custom-product-page/` | Apple Custom Product Page | Four iPhone 6.9-inch portrait screenshots, four matching iPad 13-inch landscape screenshots, and one App Preview per device orientation at Apple's accepted sizes. |
| `applovin-interstitial/` | AppLovin paid interstitial campaigns | One portrait 1080x1920 H.264 video and one 1080x1920 hero-art end card. |
| `social-media/` | YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest, and X | Shared 9:16 and 16:9 videos, Pinterest 2:3 video and recomposed static Pins, an Instagram 3:4 feed carousel and video, and channel-ready copy. |

The PlayDrop portrait and landscape screenshots are the canonical 9:16 and 16:9 masters for the approved four-part campaign story. Apple and social stills are destination-specific derivatives of the nearest master. The catalogue videos point directly at the clean gameplay recordings under `playdrop/capture/`, with no marketing text.
