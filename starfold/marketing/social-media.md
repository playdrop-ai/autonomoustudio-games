# Starfold social media launch plan

## Goal

Introduce Starfold to puzzle-game players, prove its distinctive whole-row and whole-column swipe mechanic, and convert interested viewers into free browser players.

Primary player promise:

> One swipe can trigger a massive chain reaction.

Supporting story:

1. Swipe rows and columns to create matches.
2. Fight back the spreading Ash.
3. Build increasingly large chain reactions.
4. Chase a higher score.

## Launch order

These steps happen in order. No social post may be published until the first three are complete.

1. Add `autonomoustudio/starfold` to PlayDrop's curated Featured Games list and place it at position 1.
2. Verify the public Starfold listing is live, playable while signed out, and showing the approved subtitle, description, tags, hero art, screenshots, and gameplay video.
3. Open the public PlayDrop Home and confirm Starfold is the first Featured Game.
4. Verify the destination URL and its UTM parameters in a signed-out browser.
5. Start Starfold from that browser session and confirm GA4 receives `game_played` with `game_id=autonomoustudio/starfold`.
6. Present the exact account, media, copy, destination, visibility, and publication time for Olivier's approval.
7. Publish one platform at a time, capture the public post URL, and verify the published result before continuing.
8. Record the first performance snapshot after 24 hours and a second after 72 hours.
9. Review Pinterest again after 30 days.

## Publishing rules

- Do not use third-party publishing or scheduling services.
- Publish only after Olivier explicitly approves the exact post.
- Use publishing methods in this order:
  1. Provider website
  2. Provider Android app on the connected physical phone
  3. Provider API only when the existing integration is already audited and verified to publish publicly
- Do not change an account type, profile link, approved copy, media, visibility, or timing without explicit approval.
- After publication, record the public post URL and a screenshot.

## Destination and attribution

Base destination:

`https://www.playdrop.ai/creators/autonomoustudio/apps/game/starfold`

Every destination URL must use:

- `utm_medium=organic_social`
- `utm_campaign=starfold_launch`
- a platform-specific `utm_source`
- a stable surface-specific or post-specific `utm_content`

Do not use a third-party URL shortener.

## Copy and discovery rules

- Every conversion caption must say that Starfold is free, plays in the browser, and needs no download.
- Use searchable genre phrases naturally: `match 3`, `puzzle game`, `chain reaction`, and `satisfying`.
- Use `#PuzzleGame #Match3 #OddlySatisfying #CasualGames #IndieGame` where five hashtags are appropriate.
- Use only `#PuzzleGame #Match3 #OddlySatisfying` where a shorter set is better.
- Use no hashtags on the first X post or on Pinterest.
- Keep the hook first. Do not turn captions into keyword lists.
- Do not claim that a link is in the bio unless the published profile visibly contains that clickable link.

## Measurement

For every published item, record:

- platform, public URL, publication time, media, and final copy
- `utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content`
- views or impressions
- video completion or average watch time when available
- likes, comments, shares, saves, and profile visits
- outbound clicks when the provider exposes them
- GA4 visits and `game_played` events by `utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content`
- PlayDrop attributed users and native initial game engagement when available

Review most platforms at 24 hours and 72 hours. Review Pinterest again after 30 days because its search and recommendation traffic develops more slowly. Use the results to decide which hook, format, and platform deserve the next post. Do not compare raw view counts across platforms as if they were equivalent.
